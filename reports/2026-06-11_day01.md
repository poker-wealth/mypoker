## 5. Honest note on timeline

The "16 weeks" in the documents is a **full-team** estimate (six specialists working in parallel).
As a solo build, the realistic calendar for the complete scope (9 games, 3 app platforms,
blockchain, 3-region deployment) is longer — on the order of 9–12 months. We keep the 16-milestone
**structure and quality gates** because that is where the real value lies; the milestone numbers
describe sequence and quality bars, not a fixed deadline. This is flagged now so expectations are
set early rather than late.
